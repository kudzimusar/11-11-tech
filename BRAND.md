# 11-11 Tech — Brand Notes

## Core idea

11-11 is expressed as **paired gateways**: repeated strokes, system boundaries and a bridge between contexts. It should feel architectural and technical rather than like a calendar date or numerology badge.

## Mission

**Turn difficult real-world problems into digital products people can trust, use and grow with.**

## Positioning

**Tokyo-built. Africa-aware. Global by design.**

## Operating line

**Build what matters. Prove what works.**

## Color system

- Near-black / midnight: `#050A12`
- Deep blue: `#07101F`
- Cobalt: `#347CFF`
- Electric cyan: `#7BDFFF`
- Violet: `#7256FF`
- November amber accent: `#F4B66B`
- Primary text: `#F4F8FF`
- Muted text: `#9EB0C9`

Blue is the dominant brand family. Amber is deliberately limited to small moments of emphasis.

## Voice

Confident, evidence-led, specific and technically literate. Do not claim a product is live, production-ready, verified or complete unless evidence supports the claim. Distinguish prototypes, active builds, client review and production surfaces.
